## Features

- **Authentication** :
- Users can securely register, log in, and log out using email/password or other authentication methods (like Google)

- **Best Selling** :
- The app highlights the top-selling fruits, allowing users to easily discover popular products.

- **Cart:Users**:
- can add fruits to their shopping cart, adjust quantities, and remove items before proceeding to checkout.

- **Check Out** :
-  A seamless checkout process where users can review their cart, enter shipping details, and complete the orders requierments.

- **Home** :
-  The main screen of the app, displaying featured products, best-sellers, and easy navigation to other app sections like product categories, cart, etc.

- **Onboarding** :
- A welcome onboarding experience for new users

- **Products** :
- Users can browse a variety of fruits with detailed product descriptions, images, and prices.

- **Profile** :
- Users can view and update their profile, including personal information(name , email ,password), past orders ,faviourate products ,notifications , change language , and change theme .

- **Splash** :
- A welcoming splash screen that appears when the app is launched, providing a branded experience as the app loads.

## Services Used
### Firebase
- **Authentication** :
- Firebase Authentication handles secure sign-ups and log-ins, including email/password authentication and third-party sign-ins like Google .

- **Cloud Firestore** :
- Firebase Firestore is used for storing and managing user data, product listings, and order history in real-time.

### Supabase
- **SubaBase Storage**:
- For storing product images, profile pictures, or any other media used in the app.

### Payment
- **PayPal**:
- PayPal payment gateway for users .
